## Why V2G-D was superseded

V2G-D had to claim Tier 4 against `0x475Fe397FA56884952D350aa9EE1c3946964BC0C`
and Tier 2 against `0x8B94A83D1AD3bD2337b1886E7962CA8E0bba9A34`. Both
EOAs were inherited from the V2F-LM PERP smoke (originally funded
via `PERP_SMOKE_BUYER_PRIVATE_KEY` / `PERP_SMOKE_SELLER_PRIVATE_KEY`,
operator-supplied per `deployments/base-sepolia.manifest.draft.json#L657`).
The operator reported on 2026-05-30 that **`PERP_SMOKE_SELLER_PRIVATE_KEY`
is missing and cannot be recovered**, which means the Tier 4 maker
private key for `0x475Fe397…BC0C` is unavailable; the V2G-C root
therefore commits to leaves that nobody can sign for.

`FeesManagerV2.claimTier` enforces `msg.sender == account` at L189 of
`src/fees/FeesManagerV2.sol`, so a third party cannot relay these
claims. The only safe path is to rotate the committed addresses by
publishing a new Merkle root.
